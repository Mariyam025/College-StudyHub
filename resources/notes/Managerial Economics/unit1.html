Managerial Economics - Unit 1

1. Meaning of Economics

Economics is the study of how individuals and organizations use limited resources to satisfy unlimited wants.

2. Meaning of Managerial Economics

Managerial Economics is the application of economic theories and principles to business decision-making.

3. Nature of Managerial Economics

  • Decision-oriented
  • Practical in nature
  • Based mainly on microeconomics
  • Uses economic theories
  • Helps in business planning

4. Scope of Managerial Economics

  • Demand analysis
  • Demand forecasting
  • Production analysis
  • Cost analysis
  • Pricing decisions
  • Profit planning
  • Market analysis

5. Importance

  • Helps managers make better decisions.
  • Helps in optimum use of resources.
  • Helps in pricing decisions.
  • Helps in demand forecasting.
  • Helps in profit planning.

6. Microeconomics

Microeconomics studies individual consumers, firms, products and markets.

7. Macroeconomics

Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole, including national income, inflation, unemployment and economic growth.

8. Microeconomics vs Macroeconomics

  • Micro: Individual units
  • Macro: Entire economy
  • Micro: Demand of a product
  • Macro: National income

9. Opportunity Cost

Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative that is given up when a particular choice is made.

10. Basic Economic Problem

The basic economic problem exists because resources are limited while human wants are unlimited.

  • What to produce?
  • How to produce?
  • For whom to produce?
