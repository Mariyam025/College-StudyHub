Managerial Economics - Unit 4

1. Meaning of Cost

Cost is the amount of resources used or money spent to produce goods and services.

2. Types of Cost

  • Fixed Cost
  • Variable Cost
  • Total Cost
  • Average Cost
  • Marginal Cost
  • Opportunity Cost
  • Sunk Cost

3. Fixed Cost

Fixed cost does not change with the level of production in the short run. Example: Rent of a factory.

4. Variable Cost

Variable cost changes with the level of production. Example: Raw material cost.

5. Total Cost

Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost

6. Average Cost

Average Cost = Total Cost / Total Output

7. Marginal Cost

Marginal cost is the additional cost of producing one more unit of output.

8. Market Structure

Market structure describes the nature and degree of competition in a market.

9. Perfect Competition

  • Large number of buyers and sellers
  • Homogeneous products
  • Free entry and exit
  • Firms are price takers

10. Monopoly

Monopoly is a market structure in which a single seller supplies a product or service with strong barriers to entry.

11. Monopolistic Competition

  • Many sellers
  • Product differentiation
  • Some control over price
  • Free or relatively easy entry and exit

12. Oligopoly

Oligopoly is a market structure dominated by a small number of firms.
