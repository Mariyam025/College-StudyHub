Managerial Economics - Unit 2

1. Meaning of Demand

Demand refers to the quantity of a product that consumers are willing and able to purchase at a particular price during a given period.

2. Determinants of Demand

  • Price of the product
  • Consumer income
  • Price of related goods
  • Tastes and preferences
  • Population
  • Future expectations
  • Advertising

3. Law of Demand

Other things remaining constant, quantity demanded generally increases when price decreases and decreases when price increases.

4. Demand Function

A demand function shows the relationship between quantity demanded and its determinants.

Qd = f(P, Y, Pr, T)

  • Qd = Quantity demanded
  • P = Price
  • Y = Income
  • Pr = Price of related goods
  • T = Taste and preferences

5. Elasticity of Demand

Elasticity of demand measures how much quantity demanded changes due to a change in one of its determinants.

6. Types of Elasticity

  • Price elasticity
  • Income elasticity
  • Cross elasticity

7. Demand Forecasting

Demand forecasting means estimating future demand for a product or service.

8. Importance of Demand Forecasting

  • Production planning
  • Inventory management
  • Financial planning
  • Manpower planning
  • Pricing decisions

9. Methods of Demand Forecasting

  • Survey method
  • Expert opinion
  • Market experiment
  • Trend analysis
  • Statistical methods
