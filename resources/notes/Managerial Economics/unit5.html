Managerial Economics - Unit 5

1. Pricing

Pricing means determining the amount of money that customers have to pay for a product or service.

2. Objectives of Pricing

  • Profit maximization
  • Sales growth
  • Market share
  • Survival
  • Maintaining market position

3. Pricing Methods

  • Cost-plus pricing
  • Going-rate pricing
  • Competitive pricing
  • Rate-of-return pricing
  • Value-based pricing

4. Profit

Profit is the difference between total revenue and total cost.

Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost

5. Functions of Profit

  • Reward for risk-taking
  • Source of finance
  • Encourages innovation
  • Helps business growth
  • Indicates business performance

6. Profit Maximization

Profit maximization means choosing the level of output at which the difference between total revenue and total cost is maximum.

7. Break-Even Analysis

Break-even point is the level of sales at which total revenue equals total cost. At this point, there is neither profit nor loss.

Break-Even Point = Fixed Cost / Contribution per Unit

8. Contribution

Contribution per unit = Selling Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit

9. Inflation

Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy.

10. Effects of Inflation

  • Purchasing power decreases.
  • Cost of production may increase.
  • Prices of goods and services increase.
  • Business planning becomes more difficult.

11. Business Decision Making

Managerial economics helps managers make decisions related to demand, production, cost, pricing, investment and profit.
