Unit 3 – Final Accounts and Ratio Analysis

← Back to Subjects

1. Final Accounts

Final accounts are prepared to determine the profit or loss and financial position of a business.

2. Trading Account

Trading Account is prepared to calculate gross profit or gross loss.

Gross Profit = Net Sales − Cost of Goods Sold

3. Profit and Loss Account

Profit and Loss Account determines net profit or net loss after considering indirect expenses and incomes.

Net Profit = Gross Profit + Other Income − Indirect Expenses

4. Balance Sheet

Balance Sheet shows the financial position of a business on a particular date.

5. Common Adjustments

  • Closing Stock
  • Outstanding Expenses
  • Prepaid Expenses
  • Accrued Income
  • Bad Debts
  • Depreciation

6. Ratio Analysis

Ratio analysis is a technique used to analyze financial statements by calculating relationships between accounting figures.

7. Liquidity Ratios

Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

Quick Ratio = Quick Assets / Current Liabilities

8. Profitability Ratios

  • Gross Profit Ratio
  • Net Profit Ratio
  • Return on Capital Employed

9. Efficiency Ratios

  • Inventory Turnover Ratio
  • Debtors Turnover Ratio
  • Working Capital Turnover Ratio

10. Solvency Ratios

  • Debt-Equity Ratio
  • Proprietary Ratio
  • Interest Coverage Ratio
