1. Meaning of Financial Management

Financial Management deals with planning, obtaining, using and controlling financial resources of an organization.

2. Objectives

  • Profitability
  • Efficient use of funds
  • Proper investment decisions
  • Financial stability
  • Wealth maximization

3. Scope of Financial Management

  • Investment Decisions
  • Financing Decisions
  • Dividend Decisions
  • Working Capital Management

4. Finance Manager

A finance manager is responsible for managing financial resources and supporting financial decisions of an organization.

5. Investment Decision

Investment decision determines where and how funds should be invested.

6. Financing Decision

Financing decision determines the sources from which funds should be obtained.

7. Dividend Decision

Dividend decision deals with how much profit should be distributed to shareholders and how much should be retained.

8. Capital Structure

Capital structure refers to the combination of different sources of long-term finance used by a business.

9. Cost of Capital

Cost of capital is the required return associated with obtaining funds from different sources.

10. Working Capital

Working capital is commonly represented as:

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities
