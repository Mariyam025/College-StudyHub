1. Meaning of Accounting

Accounting is the systematic process of recording, classifying, summarizing and interpreting financial transactions of a business.

2. Objectives of Accounting

  • To maintain proper records.
  • To determine profit or loss.
  • To know the financial position.
  • To provide information for decision making.
  • To help in planning and control.

3. Book Keeping vs Accounting

8. Double Entry System

Under the double entry system, every financial transaction affects at least two accounts.

Basic rule: Total Debit = Total Credit

10. Accounting Equation

Assets = Capital + Liabilities

Example: If Capital = ₹50,000 and Liabilities = ₹20,000, then Assets = ₹70,000.
