1. Journal

Journal is the book in which business transactions are recorded first in chronological order.

2. Journal Entry

A journal entry records the debit and credit aspects of a transaction.

3. Ledger

Ledger is a book containing individual accounts where transactions are classified account-wise.

4. Posting

Transferring entries from the journal to respective ledger accounts is called posting.

5. Cash Book

Cash book records cash and bank transactions.

  • Single Column Cash Book
  • Double Column Cash Book
  • Triple Column Cash Book

6. Trial Balance

Trial Balance is a statement containing debit and credit balances of ledger accounts.

Total Debit = Total Credit

7. Objectives of Trial Balance

  • Check arithmetical accuracy.
  • Help in preparation of final accounts.
  • Summarize ledger balances.
  • Help locate certain errors.

8. Depreciation

Depreciation is the gradual reduction in the value of a fixed asset due to use, time or other factors.

9. Straight Line Method

Depreciation = (Cost − Scrap Value) / Useful Life

10. Written Down Value Method

Under WDV method, depreciation is calculated on the book value of the asset.
