1. Budget

A budget is a financial or quantitative plan prepared for a future period.

2. Objectives of Budget

  • Planning
  • Coordination
  • Control
  • Resource allocation
  • Performance evaluation

3. Budget vs Forecast

BudgetForecast
A planned target.An estimate of future events.
Used for planning and control.Used to predict future conditions.

4. Types of Budget

  • Functional Budget
  • Master Budget
  • Fixed Budget
  • Flexible Budget
  • Zero-Based Budget

5. Fixed Budget

A fixed budget is prepared for a specific level of activity and normally does not change with actual activity.

6. Flexible Budget

A flexible budget can be adjusted according to different levels of activity.

7. Zero-Based Budgeting

In zero-based budgeting, expenses are justified from a zero base rather than simply carrying forward previous budgets.

8. Budgetary Control

Budgetary control is the process of comparing actual performance with budgeted performance and taking corrective action when required.

9. Cost

Cost is the amount of resources sacrificed or expenditure incurred to obtain a product, service or benefit.

10. Cost Sheet

A cost sheet presents the different elements of cost and helps determine total cost and cost per unit.

11. Elements of Cost

  • Material
  • Labour
  • Expenses
  • Direct Cost
  • Indirect Cost

12. Break-Even Point

Break-even point is the level of sales at which total revenue equals total cost.

BEP (Units) = Fixed Cost / Contribution per Unit
