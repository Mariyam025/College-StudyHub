Fundamentals of Computer

Unit 1: Introduction and Basics of Computer

1. Introduction to Computer

Definition: A computer is an electronic device that accepts data as input, processes it according to instructions, stores it and produces useful information as output.

A computer performs different operations such as calculation, data processing, storing information and communication.

Example: When we enter marks of students into a computer, it can calculate total marks and percentage and display the result.

2. Brief History of Computers

Computers have developed from simple calculating devices to powerful modern machines.

  • Abacus: One of the earliest devices used for calculation.
  • Pascaline: A mechanical calculating machine developed by Blaise Pascal.
  • Charles Babbage: Designed the Difference Engine and Analytical Engine. He is known as the Father of Computer.
  • ENIAC: One of the early electronic general-purpose computers.
  • Modern Computers: Modern computers are faster, smaller, more reliable and capable of storing and processing large amounts of data.

3. Computer System Concept

Definition: A computer system is a combination of hardware, software, data and users that work together to perform tasks.

Basic Working of Computer

The basic working of a computer can be represented as:

Input → Processing → Output
  • Input: Data is entered into the computer.
  • Processing: Computer processes the given data.
  • Output: Result is produced.
  • Storage: Data and results can be stored for future use.

4. Characteristics of Computer

Characteristic Explanation
Speed A computer can perform a large number of operations in a very short time.
Accuracy A computer gives accurate results when correct data and instructions are provided.
Storage A computer can store a large amount of data.
Diligence A computer can perform repetitive tasks without getting tired.
Versatility A computer can perform different types of tasks.
Automation A computer can perform tasks automatically according to given instructions.

5. Capabilities of Computer

  • Fast Processing: Performs calculations and processes data quickly.
  • Large Storage: Stores a large amount of information.
  • Communication: Helps users communicate and share information.
  • Data Processing: Processes large amounts of data efficiently.
  • Repetitive Work: Performs repetitive operations without fatigue.

6. Limitations of Computer

  • A computer cannot think like a human on its own.
  • It works according to instructions given by the user.
  • Wrong input can produce wrong output.
  • A computer does not have human emotions.
  • Hardware or software failure can stop its operation.

7. Types of Computers

1. Micro Computer

A micro computer is a small computer generally designed for individual users.

Examples: Desktop computer, Laptop

2. Mini Computer

A mini computer is a medium-sized computer that can support multiple users and applications.

3. Mainframe Computer

A mainframe computer is a large and powerful computer used to process huge amounts of data and support many users.

Example: Large banking and business systems.

4. Super Computer

A super computer is an extremely powerful computer used for complex calculations and scientific applications.

Uses: Weather forecasting, scientific research, simulations and complex calculations.

Type Basic Idea Example / Use
Micro Computer Small and usually personal Desktop, Laptop
Mini Computer Medium-sized and multi-user Departmental systems
Mainframe Large-scale data processing Banking systems
Super Computer Very high processing power Scientific research
